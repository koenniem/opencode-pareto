        Near-frontier band

        When one model dominates everything, the strict frontier collapses to a couple of points. The band flags models whose score is within this many points of the best available at their price or cheaper — candidates worth a look, not equals.

        Token mix

        Every model is priced on one global mix. The default is the median of OpenCode's own published per-request token profiles — extremely cache-heavy, because agentic coding re-reads a large context every turn. Change it to see how the frontier moves.
