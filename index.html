OpenCode Pareto

OpenCode Go · cost vs capability

Which model is actually worth your $10?

Go sells monthly dollar allowances, not tokens, and the discount is different on every model. This chart prices each model by the cost of a real task — Artificial Analysis’s weighted benchmark cost — after OpenCode’s prices and the plan’s usage multiplier. Then it asks which model gives the most capability for the money.
